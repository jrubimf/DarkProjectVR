Help fit Thief's items to the VR hand

Open the folder Thief Gold or Thief 2 is installed in. The page reads the game's own models from it, in your browser: nothing is uploaded, and no game file is hosted here.

Pick an item, move and turn it with the handles or the numbers until it sits in the hand the way Garrett would hold it, then send your fits. Each one is reviewed before it ships.

Chrome or Edge open the folder directly; other browsers ask you to pick it as an upload.
